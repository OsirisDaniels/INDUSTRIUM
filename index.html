Field Manual

Sand falls and piles up. Water flows sideways. Fire rises and burns out. Ore is heavier than water. Drills extract ore from nearby deposits, generators provide power, and furnaces refine ore into more valuable metal. Collectors recover nearby loose ore. Use the Sell button to earn credits.
